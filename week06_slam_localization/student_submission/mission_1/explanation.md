# Mission 1

## Drift And Revisit

When I returned to area that I had already explore, I noticed that some of the walls didn't line up properly. Some were doubled or shifted which could be because the robot lost track of its exact position. I can't be sure of SLAM corrected some of the drift, as I didn't compare the wall positions before and after returning to the same area. 

## Limitations

An area that remained poorly outlined was near the center of the house, where some of the walls seem to overlap. I think this happened because the walls and obstacles blocked the LiDAR from scanning this area properly. Also, since this was the biggest area, I think going over it multiple times would have helped to map it better.

## Route.Original Prediction

I plan to first explore the right side of the house, starting from the bottom-right area and then moving towards the top-right area. After that, I want to explore the left side of the house, once again starting with the bottom area and then moving towards the top area which also happens to be the closest area to the robot's starting position. Finally, I plan to return to the robot's start position as I want to compare new LiDAR readings with the original ones to see whether the previously mapped walls still align correctly and if there is any drift in the robot's estimated position. I predict that map coverage may be weaker around corners and narrow passages.
