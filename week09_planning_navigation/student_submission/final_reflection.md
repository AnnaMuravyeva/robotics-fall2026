## Response

What stood out to me the most about this lab was seeing how differently the robot performed depending on the planner we used. I found it especially interesting that even though DFS was able to find a path, the robot still failed to reach its goal because the path was hard to follow. I didn't see this coming at all. I also liked the last mission because it made me think about how a robot needs to consider the people around it, not just obstacles. So sometimes it might be a better choice to take a longer route if it gives people more personal space.
